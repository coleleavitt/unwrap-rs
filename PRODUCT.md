# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring teams, security practitioners, technical peers, and potential collaborators evaluating Cole Leavitt's work and looking for a direct way to connect.

## Product Purpose

Establish Cole's professional identity as a principal security engineer and security researcher, demonstrate technical craft through a distinctive Rust-native interaction, and provide immediate paths to his public work and contact details.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, and quietly playful. The voice should feel credible to experienced engineers without becoming institutional, inflated, or impersonal.

## Anti-references

Generic card-grid portfolio templates, keyword-stuffed cybersecurity landing pages, and language that implies affiliation with organizations such as JPL. Avoid corporate filler and unsupported claims.

## Design Principles

- Lead with Cole's name and professional identity.
- Let the Rust interaction demonstrate craft instead of over-explaining it.
- Keep every claim specific, truthful, and visible on the page.
- Make GitHub, LinkedIn, and email reachable in one step.
- Preserve the compact mission-control character without obscuring basic context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve browser zoom, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, touch targets of at least 44px, and a reduced-motion experience.
